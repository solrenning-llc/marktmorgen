### Code sections the bill adds or amends (as identified in the index)

- R&TC §6016 (definition of tangible personal property): adds digital products, operative 01/01/2027.
- R&TC §6016.1 (new): "digital product" means, except as provided, prewritten computer software transferred on tangible storage media, transferred electronically, or accessed remotely; excludes digital assets, digital audio works, digital audiovisual works, digital books, digital infrastructure, digital video game products and digital visual works. **The index warned that an earlier version of the bill listed fewer exclusions;** the enrolled list must be read in full.
- R&TC §6016.2 (new): defines "accessed remotely" and "transferred electronically".
- R&TC §6052 and §6201.55 (cited by CDTFA's retailer guide for the $5,000,000 per-purchaser threshold, section 3 below).
- Government Code §53084.5 (amended; local tax point, not read).
- Bill section 34: CDTFA may adopt emergency regulations to implement sections 1 through 20 of the bill.

The full list of sections in the chaptered text was not read; this list is incomplete by construction.

## 4. Thresholds: the queue mixes two different figures

| Figure | What it is, per the official index | Source | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| USD 500,000 (R&TC §6203(c)(4)) | Remote seller registration and collection: total combined sales of tangible personal property for delivery in California by the retailer and related persons above USD 500,000 in the preceding or current calendar year ([CDTFA Wayfair page](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/industry/wayfair.htm), [§6203](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/lawguides/vol1/sutl/6203.html)). | Do digital products count toward the USD 500,000? | Not stated in any extract seen. Since §6016 makes a digital product tangible personal property from 01/01/2027, counting them is the natural reading, but no official sentence saying so was read. Do not assume they are left out. | — | Unconfirmed (open) |
| USD 5,000,000 | **Not a registration threshold.** It is a per-purchaser relief: when a retailer's sales of electronically delivered or remotely accessed digital products **to the same purchaser** exceed USD 5,000,000 in calendar year 2027 (from 01/01/2028, the current or preceding year), the retailer may be relieved and that purchaser self-assesses use tax; not for insurers; CDTFA may still require the retailer to collect if the purchaser obtains a waiver; adjusted by 10/01/2031 and every five years (R&TC §6052, §6201.55, proposed Reg. 1600.1). | CDTFA Retailers guide, L-1036 / index 11/15/2026 | Consistent, full text unread |
| Registration | L-1036: in-state sellers need a seller's permit; out-of-state retailers engaged in business in California register for a Certificate of Registration – Use Tax. Place of sale for local tax is generally the customer's address. | CDTFA L-1036 | Consistent, full text unread |

A Marktmorgen plan costs at most USD 9,990 a year, far below USD 5,000,000 per purchaser, so the per-purchaser relief would not move the tax from Marktmorgen to the customer in any realistic case. This is arithmetic on today's price list, not a legal finding.

It is a consumption tax and must not be confused with, or added on top of, the 13.3% on profit already in the California profile, nor with the remote seller threshold.

## 6. Effect on TX point 10 (Marktmorgen's own plans) — open for part (b)

TX point 10 says: sales tax only in states that tax subscription software and where Marktmorgen's sales pass the state's economic nexus threshold, collected outside the price. On what the official index shows, California joins the states that tax SaaS from 01/01/2027. Open questions:

1. Do digital products count toward the USD 500,000 §6203(c)(4) total (section 4)? Until read, the counter cannot exclude them.
2. Which address sources the local rate for a remotely accessed plan, and what evidence is kept (proposed Reg. 1600.2/1600.3 drafts; L-1036 "customer's address")?
3. Annual plans billed before 01/01/2027 for months after it: transition rule not read.
4. Free trials: the meeting materials record an open stakeholder question whether a free trial is a "purchase" without consideration; no CDTFA answer found.

Nothing here turns collection on, changes `lib/receiving-costs.ts`, the California profile of PV1(k), `docs/tax-uncertainty.md`, `docs/margin-alignment-2026-10-18.md` or any test; that is part (b).

## 7. Effect on TX point 11 (creators' sales, marketplace facilitator) — open for part (c)

## Divergences from the queue's investigation points

1. Regulation 1502.2 is not "Computers, Programs, and Data Processing"; that is Regulation 1502 (to be retitled "Data Processing"). Proposed 1502.2 is "Custom Computer Software".
2. Regulations 1600, 1600.1, 1600.2, 1600.3 and 1502.2 are proposed emergency regulations, not adopted, as of the index on 11/15/2026.
3. The USD 5,000,000 figure is a per-purchaser liability shift, not the remote seller registration threshold, which remains USD 500,000 under §6203(c)(4) as far as the index shows.
4. The effective date of the statute (06/29/2026) differs from the operative date of the digital products change (01/01/2027).
5. None of the bill number, chapter, date or content could be read in full: the primary hosts were not reachable from this session. The queue's bill citation is consistent with the official index but stays unconfirmed until read.
