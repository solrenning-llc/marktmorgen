## 6. Effect on TX point 10 (Marktmorgen's own plans) — open for part (b)

TX point 10 says: sales tax only in states that tax subscription software and where Marktmorgen's sales pass the state's economic nexus threshold, collected outside the price. On what CDTFA publishes, California joins the states that tax SaaS from 01/01/2027. Open questions:

1. Do digital products count toward the USD 500,000 §6203(c)(4) total (section 4)? No CDTFA sentence found; the counter cannot exclude them.
2. Local rate: the purchaser's known address in the order billing, shipping, payment instrument, mailing (§6010.5(b) per S3; Retailers guide). The evidence to keep for it, and for the out-of-state relief of §6054 ("reasonable effort to obtain accurate and complete address information"), must be designed in part (b).
3. Annual plans billed before 01/01/2027 for months after it: only proposed Reg. 1600(f)(2)(B) answers (payments for periods on or after 01/01/2027 taxed); the statute's own rule and the final regulation were not read.
4. Free trials: open (section 2).

Nothing here turns collection on, changes `lib/receiving-costs.ts`, the California profile of PV1(k), `docs/tax-uncertainty.md`, `docs/margin-alignment-2026-10-18.md` or any test; that is part (b).

## 7. Effect on TX point 11 (creators' sales, marketplace facilitator) — open for part (c)

1. The excluded categories of §6016.1(b) (digital books, digital audio, audiovisual and visual works, per S3 and L-1036) appear to cover much of what creators sell (e-books, recorded courses, music, downloads of artwork); creator software, templates or apps that are prewritten software would not be excluded. Each product type needs classification against the definitions in §6016.1(c), read in the chaptered text.
2. CDTFA's digital products Retailers guide (S7) says: "Marketplace facilitators that facilitate sales of digital products transferred electronically or accessed remotely made by marketplace sellers on the marketplace facilitators' platform may need to register for a seller's permit or a Certificate of Registration - Use Tax. For more information, see our Tax Guide for Marketplace Facilitator Act." That guide (S12) still speaks only of "tangible merchandise" and gives no digital rule; its counting rule is that a seller's USD 500,000 includes "sales facilitated through a marketplace facilitator's marketplace", and a registered facilitator "is the retailer selling or making the sale of the tangible personal property sold through its marketplace". **Confirmed (CDTFA, read in full):** CDTFA says facilitators of digital products may need to register. **Open:** whether and how Marketplace Facilitator Act liability attaches to digital products, and whether Marktmorgen is a "marketplace facilitator" under that Act for creators' direct charges.
3. A public comment (S5) asked "How will the $5mil spend direct pay requirements interact with the marketplace facilitator rules?"; no CDTFA answer was found.
4. Link these to questions 20 and 23 to 27 of `docs/tax-uncertainty.md` in part (c), without building anything new beyond the record and the calculation.

## Divergences from the queue's investigation points

1. Regulation 1502.2 is not "Computers, Programs, and Data Processing"; that is the title of current Regulation 1502 (proposed to become "Data Processing"). Proposed 1502.2 is "Custom Computer Software". Read in full (S4, S10).
2. Regulations 1600, 1600.1, 1600.2, 1600.3 and 1502.2 are proposed emergency regulations still listed as a current informal rulemaking topic on 11/15/2026; none is in force. Read in full (S6, S4, S3).
3. The USD 5,000,000 figure is a per-purchaser liability shift, not the remote seller registration threshold, which remains USD 500,000 under §6203(c)(4). Read in full (S9, S7, S3).
