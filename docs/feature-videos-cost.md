# Short product videos on the feature pages: cost and ceiling (PROPOSAL, 241 [CF4], unit 1)

**PROPOSAL.** Nothing is filmed, uploaded or shown by this unit, and nothing changes a price, a limit, a flag or a gate. The ceiling below is for the owner to decide. Until it is built, no feature page carries a video.

Queue item 241 [CF4] asks for short screen videos, recorded by browser automation on the demo store (Harbor Kitchen, labeled as fictional), showing each main feature working for real:
- no actors, no invented figures and no switched-off features;
- with captions and a text transcript;
- loaded only when asked, so the page's first paint is not slowed;
- hosted on the video service already in use (Bunny Stream, `lib/stream.ts`), with an automatic ceiling and the cost counted among the company's fixed costs (FX275).

This unit answers the cost and the ceiling. It is generated by `node scripts/feature-videos-cost.mjs` from `tests/feature-videos-cost-table.ts`, and `tests/feature-videos-cost.test.ts` fails when the two differ.

Delivery is priced at the dearest region, never at the "from $0.005" the service advertises: a "from" price is not a ceiling. Which network the library is on is the open queue item on video delivery, not settled here.

## How the ceiling holds

- **Plays are counted before a video is asked for.** Each feature page counts a play against the month's ceiling before it loads the player. Past the ceiling, the page shows the still picture and the full transcript, which say the same thing, and no video is requested. The cost can then never pass the month's figure above.
- **Nothing loads until it is asked for.** The page draws a still picture with a play button. The player and the video load only when the button is pressed, so the first paint and the page's weight are unchanged.
- **Unknown stops the video.** If the count cannot be read exactly, the page shows the transcript, never the video.
- **Replays inside one player are the open point.** A play is counted when the player loads, but a visitor can replay inside it. The player's link is signed (`BUNNY_STREAM_TOKEN_KEY`, `lib/stream.ts`) and can expire soon after the video's length. Bunny's page on it (https://bunny.net/docs/stream-embed-token-authentication, read on 11 November 2026) says "every embed request to the iframe must include a valid `token` and `expires` pair" and that after `expires` "the URL will no longer be accessible". It says nothing about the video's own pieces or about replays. So whether the expiry stops a replay of what the browser already holds, and how many bytes a replay fetches again, is unknown until it is measured in the next unit. Until then, the ceiling above is not a proven bound, and nothing is built on it.

The counter and the page are a later unit, with their own tests.

## What is unknown, and so never zero

- **The storage price per region at its dearest.** The service shows only "from $0.01/GB". The figure above uses the repository's own `STREAM_STORAGE_PER_GB` for every one of the 8 regions. It is small next to delivery, but it is not a dated worst price.
- **Captions and transcripts.** They are written from the recorded steps, not by a paid caption service, so they add no cost. If a paid service were used, its price per minute would be added here first.
- **Recording.** It runs on the project's own browser automation, against a local demo store, at no provider cost.
- **Which features can be filmed working for real today.** Anything that needs a paid checkout cannot be, while `PLANS_ON_SALE` is false. A video of it would show a switched-off feature, which the item forbids. Those pages get their video only after opening. The list is the next unit, with the recorder.

## Fixed cost (FX275)

The month above is a company cost, not any one customer's. It goes into the fixed costs that the minimum base of paying customers covers. Below that base, MR asks for the fixed costs to be as low as possible, so the ceiling can be set lower, or the videos left off, until the base is reached.
