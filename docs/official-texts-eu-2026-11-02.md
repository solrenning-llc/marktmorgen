## Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) — for 619 [SZ3]

Source: EUR-Lex, consolidated text CELEX 02016R0679-20160504, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A02016R0679-20160504 (read 02/11/2026).

- Art. 12(3): the controller answers a request under Articles 15 to 22 "without undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt of the request"; the period "may be extended by two further months where necessary, taking into account the complexity and number of the requests", and the person must be told of the extension, with the reasons, within the first month. A request made electronically is answered electronically where possible.
- Art. 12(4): if the controller does not act, it tells the person within one month why, and about the right to complain to a supervisory authority and to seek a judicial remedy.
- Art. 12(5): answers are "free of charge". Where requests are "manifestly unfounded or excessive, in particular because of their repetitive character", the controller may charge a reasonable fee based on administrative costs or refuse; "The controller shall bear the burden of demonstrating the manifestly unfounded or excessive character of the request."
- Art. 15(3): "The controller shall provide a copy of the personal data undergoing processing." For further copies a reasonable fee based on administrative costs may be charged; an electronic request gets "a commonly used electronic form".
- Art. 15(4): the right to a copy "shall not adversely affect the rights and freedoms of others" (why other people's comments stay out of a buyer's copy, C3-7).
- Art. 20(1): the right to receive the personal data the person provided "in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format" and to transmit it to another controller, under the conditions listed in that paragraph.

Applied in C3-5 and C3-7: every request is answered; the cost sits on a funded company exposure; one fresh copy per person per 30 days per store, a repeat receives the copy already made; a spent ceiling queues the request inside the legal period instead of refusing it.

## Council Directive (EU) 2021/514 (DAC7) — for 625 [HN1]

Source: EUR-Lex, CELEX 32021L0514, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32021L0514 (read 02/11/2026; Annex V, Section I, and Article 2).

- "Platform Operator" means an Entity that contracts with Sellers to make available all or part of a Platform to such Sellers.
- "Reporting Platform Operator": any Platform Operator other than an Excluded Platform Operator that is either tax-resident, incorporated, managed or has a permanent establishment in a Member State, or has none of those ties but facilitates Relevant Activities by Reportable Sellers or the rental of immovable property located in a Member State; Qualified Non-Union Platform Operators are excluded. (Paraphrase of the two situations; check the exact wording of points (a) and (b) before citing.)
- "Relevant Activity": an activity carried out for Consideration that is immovable property rental, a Personal Service, the sale of Goods or the rental of any mode of transport; activities of a Seller acting as an employee of the Platform Operator are excluded.
- "Excluded Seller" includes a Seller that facilitated fewer than 30 Relevant Activities for the sale of Goods with total Consideration not exceeding EUR 2,000 in the Reporting Period, besides Governmental Entities, listed entities and large property-rental sellers.
- Application: Member States apply the provisions from 1 January 2023 (Article 2).

Open for HN1: whether a creator store's digital products and live calls are "sale of Goods" or "Personal Services" under Annex V, and whether Marktmorgen, with direct charges on the creator's own Stripe account, is a Platform Operator that "contracts with Sellers to make available" a Platform in the sense above. Not decided here.
