## Records and qualified retention

Section 382-5(1) requires explanatory transaction records for its covered dealings. Its retention period is the longest of five years after completion of the relevant transaction or act, the applicable assessment review period, and the refreshed review period specified for an amendment. Subsection (4) separately covers elections and calculations, including their basis and method; subsection (8) requires English or accessible conversion to English and records sufficient to ascertain liabilities and entitlements.

Subsections (11)-(12) qualify the record obligations for specified purposes where the Commissioner gives notice or a company is finally dissolved. Ordinary store closure is not proof of final dissolution. The operational effect of any exception for this LLC remains unreviewed; no deletion schedule is inferred. This document does not change any retention in code or claim that compliant recordkeeping is implemented.
