## Conditional annual tax and effective years

The FTB's [Limited liability company page](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html) says an LLC doing business in California or organized or registered there is generally subject to an annual tax of USD 800. It places the payment on the 15th day of the 4th month and identifies FTB 3522 as the LLC Tax Voucher. The page also requires Form 568 and points an LLC with income or loss inside and outside California to Schedule R for apportionment and allocation. None of those statements decides whether Solrenning LLC has California nexus, registration, California-source receipts, or a filing obligation.

The [2025 Form 568 booklet, revised March 2026](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.html), likewise describes the annual tax for an LLC doing business in California or accepted or registered by the California Secretary of State. That booklet is preparation guidance and expressly says its instructions are not authoritative law. It does not replace a facts-and-law review for this LLC.

The effective year matters. The FTB page records a first-year exception for tax years beginning in 2021 through 2023; that historical window is not a current 2026 exemption. The FTB's [2026 tax-form changes page](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/whats-new.html) separately reports a prospective first-taxable-year reduction from USD 800 to USD 400 for an LP, LLP or LLC required to file in California in tax years beginning in 2027 through 2029. This prospective first-taxable-year reduction is not applied to 2026, does not prove that this LLC has a California first taxable year, and does not reduce any complete operating-cost ceiling before applicability and timing are reviewed.

The FTB's [LLC filing publication](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/misc/3556.html) describes a narrow 15-day exception when the LLC conducts no business in California and its taxable year is 15 days or less. It also describes short-form cancellation and a deployed-military exception. No exception is presumed here.

## Graduated LLC fee and payment timing

The FTB sources distinguish the annual tax from the LLC fee based on total income derived from or attributable to California. The filing publication describes that total as gross income plus cost of goods sold paid or incurred in connection with the business, with California sourcing determined under the cited assignment and apportionment rules. The actual receipts, costs of goods sold, classifications and sourcing facts for Marktmorgen have not been reviewed.

The official schedule is:

| Total California income, rounded to whole dollars | LLC fee |
| --- | ---: |
| USD 250,000 through USD 499,999 | USD 900 |
| USD 500,000 through USD 999,999 | USD 2,500 |
| USD 1,000,000 through USD 4,999,999 | USD 6,000 |
| USD 5,000,000 or more | USD 11,790 |

The FTB page requires the estimated fee by the 15th day of the 6th month of the current taxable year and identifies FTB 3536. The [FTB penalty reference chart, revised January 2026](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/misc/1024.html) lists an underpayment penalty of 10% of the underpayment and a safe harbor tied to 100% of the fee for the prior taxable year. These observations do not choose a due date, fee tier, safe harbor, extension, return position or payment method for this LLC.
