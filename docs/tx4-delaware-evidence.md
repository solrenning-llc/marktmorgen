## LLC tax, timing and late-payment exposure

The Division's instructions say no Annual Report is required for these LLCs; this does not remove the annual tax. They specify no proration and assessment when active at any time from 1 January through 31 December. An LLC tax is not a corporation's annual-report obligation.

Section 18-1107(c) makes the annual tax due on 1 June following the close of the calendar year, or upon cancellation; the current year's full tax is due before the cancellation filing when a certificate terminates the entity. The calendar year being assessed and the later due date are different. Nothing here establishes that this LLC was active in a particular year or has already paid.

Section 18-1107(e) imposes USD 200 for the LLC's late or missing annual tax. Section 18-1107(c) provides 1.5% interest for each month or portion until paid; the Division's instructions explain interest on tax and penalty. These are contingent exposures, not an approved allowance for paying late or a ceiling for all noncompliance. Registered-series charges and penalties must not be silently folded into the ordinary LLC figure if they apply.
