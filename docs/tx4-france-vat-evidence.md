## Conditional local returns and representation

The 2025 registration page distinguishes customs-only registration from operations requiring CA3 returns. It describes reverse charge for a foreign non-established supplier invoicing a customer identified for French VAT. Office and fiscal representative requirements depend on establishment, the listed mutual-assistance countries and the payroll-withholding branch. A mandatory representative and an optional agent are different arrangements. This document does not decide which branch applies to the LLC or infer that selling to a French buyer alone fixes the answer.

For foreign businesses under the SIEE categories described in the older reporting page, EM is monthly and ET is quarterly under a stated annual-VAT condition; both call for a nil return when there are no taxable operations. The seasonal ES category instead requires no return when there is no taxable transaction. That page describes electronic filing by the 19th of the following month or month after the quarter, as applicable. This document does not select the LLC's regime or competent office. The older distance-sales threshold on that page is not imported as a current threshold. Actual account dates, payment instructions, calendar adjustments, corrective returns and current eligibility must be checked separately; no due-date automation is authorized.

## Records: distinct periods and start points

The 2026 Service Public summary distinguishes ten years for invoices/accounting evidence from six years for fiscal documents. The underlying provisions must be matched to the actual record and business:

- L102 B I generally measures six years from the last operation in the books/registers or document creation. Computer-created or received covered records remain electronic for that period. Its special OSS/IOSS-register rule measures ten years from 31 December of the transaction year. Its other paragraphs and commencement provisions must not be replaced by a single global expiry.
- L123-22 requires covered accounting documents in euros and French, with accounting documents/supporting evidence kept ten years. This document does not impose a universal French-language or euro-book rule on the LLC without reviewing whether that commercial-law duty applies.
- BOFiP section I explains ten-year OSS/IOSS registers and prompt electronic production on request. Section II separately addresses electronic interfaces, including facilitators not treated as buyer-resellers. Ordinary accounting remains relevant outside the special schemes. A platform is not exempt from all record work merely because another party pays VAT; this does not decide the LLC's platform status or change art. 9a.

These are review inputs, not a deletion schedule. Store closure does not establish that all legal periods or evidence obligations ended; accessible retrieval may still be needed after closure. This document does not change retention, storage location or deletion code, and it does not certify an implemented compliant archive. Cross-border access, privacy/lawful retention, which records are necessary and overlapping obligations remain for the complete review.
