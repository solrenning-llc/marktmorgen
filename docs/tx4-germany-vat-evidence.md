## Dated primary evidence

1. **Ordinary local returns: UStG section 18 and ELSTER's 2025 form guidance.**

   https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__18.html

   https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_fsekapg_202501

   Section 18(1) describes electronic advance returns and the corresponding payment by the tenth day after the reporting period. Section 18(2) distinguishes quarterly and monthly periods; section 18(3) describes an annual return, subject to its express special-scheme and exemption qualifications. Periods, startup rules, extensions and the actual account calendar must be checked for the applicable facts. Neither an annual-return deadline nor a filing frequency is assigned to the LLC here.

   Direct individual-page requests for section 18 timed out; the official indexed text was readable and the basic electronic-filing statement was cross-checked against ELSTER's 2025 guidance. That guidance is for a German company-registration form, not proof that this foreign LLC fits that form. No older form's monetary thresholds are imported.

2. **Non-Union OSS is a distinct route: UStG sections 18i and 16(1c), and BZSt.**

   https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__18i.html

   https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__16.html

   https://online.portal.bzst.de/SharedDocs/Leistungsbeschreibung/EN/besteuerungsverfahren_one-stop-shop_fuer_nicht_EU_unternehmen.html

   Section 18i concerns the special scheme for qualifying services by businesses not established in the EU; its participation is uniform across the covered EU services. Section 16(1c) defines the quarter. Under section 18i(3), the return is due within one month and payment by the last day of the following month; corrections within the specified three-year window belong in a later return identifying the original period. Crucially, section 18(1) through 18(4) do not apply to the covered German supplies under that special scheme. This prevents counting an ordinary local return as an automatic additional duty for those same supplies.

   BZSt explains the conditional non-EU option, a single return in the chosen identification member state for covered transactions, electronic records and later-return corrections. On the consultation date, its public description labelled the linked forms as temporarily unavailable; this note does not assert that filing can be completed. It does not choose Germany as identification state, enroll the LLC or establish that all platform transactions qualify. Existing [OSS evidence](tx4-oss-evidence.md) remains separate and unchanged.

3. **Invoices: UStG sections 14b and 27(40).**

   https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__14b.html

   https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__27.html

   Section 14b(1) describes eight years for its specified invoice copies and received invoices, starting at the end of the calendar year in which the invoice was issued; its cross-reference to AO section 147(3) remains material. Section 27(40) applies the version effective from 1 January 2025 to invoices whose retention had not expired on 31 December 2024, with a separate 1 January 2026 qualification for specified supervised institutions. Those are source rules, not an assessment that the LLC is such an institution.

   Section 14b's separate storage-location and electronic-access provisions also require the actual facts and cross-references to be checked. No particular storage country, permission, universal deletion date or accounting-record retention period is approved here.

4. **Special-scheme transaction records: UStG section 22(1).**

   https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__22.html

   For its specified special-scheme cases, including section 18i, section 22(1) describes ten years from the end of the year in which the transaction or business operation occurred, and electronic availability to the competent authority on request. This note does not shorten those separate OSS transaction records to eight years by borrowing the invoice rule. It does not decide which actual records or lawful personal-data retention the LLC requires. The retention implementation remains unchanged.

The consultation date is not a source publication or commencement date.
