## Primary guidance and applicability boundary

Its business-only exclusion concerns services supplied to GST-registered New Zealand businesses for their business use. ## Recurring returns and filing evidence

The remote-services page describes quarterly filing. [Inland Revenue's frequency guidance](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/changing-your-filing-frequency-or-accounting-basis/changing-your-gst-filing-frequency), updated 31 March 2025, limits the relevant quarterly category to non-residents supplying only remote services, low-value imported goods or listed services. This is not a universal monthly, two-monthly or six-monthly choice. Other supplies require reassessment; this document selects no filing frequency for the LLC.

[Inland Revenue: filing GST](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/filing-and-paying-gst-and-refunds/filing-gst), updated 23 June 2021 and read on the evidence date, says each taxable period needs a return, including a nil return. Its general due-date rule is the 28th of the following month, with periods ending 31 March due on 7 May and periods ending 30 November due on 15 January. Payment falls on the same day as the return; the guidance says there is no extension of time to file. These are guidance rules, not a verified working-day adjustment or the actual account calendar. Confirm the applicable period and current due date before any filing. This document schedules nothing.

Budget inference, not a provider quote: classification review, reconciliation, preparation, review, timely submission and a retained receipt can recur even without sales. Corrections, follow-up and qualified assistance may add work. No filing is prepared or delivered here.
