## Overseas-vendor classification evidence

IRAS's [overseas-businesses guidance](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-%28gst%29/gst-and-digital-economy/overseas-businesses), read on the evidence date, describes the overseas vendor registration (OVR) regime for B2C supplies of remote services and low-value goods to Singapore. It says an overseas business must register when its annual global turnover exceeds S$1 million and its annual B2C supplies of remote services and/or low-value goods to customers in Singapore exceed S$100,000. Both thresholds, the applicable measurement periods and the actual supply totals require current records; this summary does not establish that either threshold is met.

The same guidance defines B2C supplies as supplies to non-GST registered persons and B2B imports as supplies to GST-registered persons. For remote services, the overseas supplier treats the customer as non-GST registered by default unless the customer provides a GST registration number. That administrative rule does not validate a number or decide the customer's actual status. This evidence does not classify Marktmorgen's supplies, customers or marketplace role.

For customer belonging, the guidance says an overseas supplier may maintain two pieces of non-conflicting evidence using appropriate proxies, with billing address, IP address and credit card information as examples. It does not make every proxy conclusive and does not decide what Marktmorgen currently possesses or may lawfully collect. ## Supplier and marketplace boundaries

IRAS says that, under certain conditions, an electronic marketplace operator may be regarded as the supplier of B2C remote services made by overseas suppliers through the marketplace. A deemed operator must include the relevant marketplace supplies when determining its registration liability and, once registered, account for those supplies in addition to its own direct supplies. The facts and conditions that make an operator the supplier are not resolved merely because software hosts a transaction.

IRAS's [GST-return guidance](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-%28gst%29/filing-gst/completing-gst-returns) says an operator supplying remote services subject to GST for third-party suppliers identifies that role in Box 15 and includes the relevant value in the standard-rated-supplies box. It also notes an expanded non-digital-services scope from 1 January 2023. This evidence does not decide that Marktmorgen is an electronic marketplace operator for GST, assign any third-party supply to it, or replace transaction-level reconciliation of direct and marketplace supplies.

The overseas-businesses guidance describes OVR as a simplified pay-only regime, with electronic filing and no input-tax claims under that regime. Those are conditional consequences, not a selected registration route. This work does not register, charge, collect, file or pay GST, and it does not choose an adviser, representative, return method or customer-status control.
